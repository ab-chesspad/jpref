# DroidPref Privacy Policy

*Effective date: October 28, 2026*

DroidPref is a preferans card game for Android. You play against computer opponents on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Summary

- DroidPref does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any account.
- It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.
- Your games and settings stay on your device.
- Information leaves the device only if **you** choose to send a game log, as described below.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone or tablet:

- your settings, such as language and game rules;
- the current game and score;
- a game log, which records deals, bids and moves, and any errors;
- a random identifier created when the app first runs. It is not linked to your device, your Google account or any other identifier.

This data is not sent anywhere automatically. Android's own backup feature may include it in your device backup, according to your device's backup settings; such backups are handled by Google, not by DroidPref.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Sending a game log (optional)

To help fix problems, you can send a game log to the developer. This happens only when you:

- press **Submit Log** and confirm, or
- answer **Yes** when the app asks to send a log after an error.

A submitted log contains:

- the record of your games: cards, bids, moves and scores;
- technical details of any error that occurred;
- your Android version number and the number of processor cores;
- the random identifier described above, used to tell submitted logs apart;
- any comment you choose to type before sending.

The log does not contain your name, account details, contacts, location or any other personal information, unless you type it into the comment yourself. Please do not include personal information in comments.

Logs are sent to the developer's server at `jpref.elementfx.com`. The connection is **not encrypted** (HTTP), so the log could be read by others on the network while it is being sent. Do not send a log if this concerns you.

Submitted logs are used only to find and fix bugs and improve the game's play. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Submitted logs are kept for one month and then deleted.

## Permissions

DroidPref asks for internet access and network-state access only so that it can send a game log when you ask it to and check for a connection before doing so. The game itself works fully offline.

## Children

DroidPref is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- You can play without ever sending a log.
- To have a submitted log deleted, write to [ab.jpref@gmail.com](mailto:ab.jpref@gmail.com). Include the log file name the app showed after sending, if you have it.
